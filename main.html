Our Mission

Nurturing change from the ground up

Information is the first step toward conservation. We translate climate resources into regional Ghanaian languages and organise on-campus environmental action that anyone can join.

Translate

We turn dense climate science into Twi, Ga and Ewe so knowledge reaches every community, not just English speakers.

Restore

Community-led tree planting, campus clean-ups and waste-management workshops that put restoration into practice.

Empower

We equip KNUST students to become confident, global climate advocates through training, seminars and fellowships.
