Global Translation Reach

Nurturing Change from the Ground Up

At Climate Cardinals KNUST, we believe that information is the first step toward conservation. We are a youth-led chapter dedicated to translating climate resources into regional Ghanaian languages and organizing on-campus environmental initiatives.

  • check_circle Community-led tree planting and waste management workshops.
  • check_circle Resource translation into Twi, Ga, and Ewe.
  • check_circle Empowering KNUST students to become global climate advocates.
